    How to Replace the Drum Unit on a Brother Printer

    The drum unit transfers toner onto the paper. It lasts several toner cartridges, and the printer counts its pages: when the count runs out you see "Drum End Soon", then "Replace Drum".

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Drum or toner?

    • Toner is the powder. It runs out first and is the part you change most often.
    • Drum carries the image to the paper. Each product page shows the drum’s page yield from our product data; for the drums below that is between 10,000 and 18,000 pages.

    Signs the drum needs replacing

    • "Drum End Soon" or "Replace Drum" on the display.
    • Faint or grey pages after a new toner.
    • Marks or spots that repeat at the same distance down every page.

    Fitting the new drum

    1. Open the front cover and lift out the drum and toner assembly.
    2. Push down the green lock lever and lift the toner cartridge out of the old drum.
    3. Unpack the new drum and fit the same toner cartridge into it until it clicks.
    4. Slide the assembly back in and close the cover.
    5. Reset the drum counter. The printer does not know you fitted a new drum until you do, and the button steps differ by model: Brother: reset the drum counter (HL-L2375DW), colour models (HL-L3240CDW), or any other model on Brother NZ support.

    Reset the counter only after fitting a new drum. If a cartridge you bought from us is faulty or still is not recognised after these checks, contact us and see our returns policy.
