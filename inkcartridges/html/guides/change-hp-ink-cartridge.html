    How to Change the Ink Cartridge in an HP Printer

    HP DeskJet, ENVY and OfficeJet printers all change cartridges the same basic way. The part that differs is which cartridge your model takes, so check the table in section 3 before you buy.

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Changing the cartridge

    1. Switch the printer on and load plain paper.
    2. Open the cartridge access door and wait until the carriage moves to the middle and stops.
    3. Push the old cartridge down gently to release it, then pull it towards you out of its slot.
    4. Take the new cartridge out of its pack and pull off the plastic tape. Do not touch the copper contacts or the nozzles.
    5. Slide the cartridge into its slot (tri-colour on the left, black on the right on most models) and push it up until it clicks.
    6. Close the door. If the printer asks, print and scan the alignment page.

    HP’s own steps for the DeskJet 2700, 4100 and 4800: HP: replacing ink cartridges.

    "Cartridge problem", "incompatible" or "not recognised"

    • Check the tape is fully removed.
    • Take the cartridge out and push it back in until it clicks.
    • Wipe the copper contacts on the cartridge with a dry, lint-free cloth.
    • Check the cartridge number matches your printer (section 3).
    • If a cartridge you bought from us is faulty or still is not recognised after these checks, contact us and see our returns policy.
