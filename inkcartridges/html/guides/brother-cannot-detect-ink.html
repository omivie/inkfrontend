    Brother Printer Says "Cannot Detect Ink": What to Check

    "Cannot Detect" means the printer cannot read a cartridge. The fix is almost always one of three things: packaging left on, a cartridge not fully seated, or the wrong cartridge code.

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Remove every protective part

    Brother cartridges ship with an orange protective part or cap and, on some, a seal. Take the cartridge out and check all of them are gone.

    Reseat the cartridge

    1. Open the ink cartridge cover.
    2. Push the lock release lever and take the cartridge out.
    3. Put it back in the slot that matches its colour label and push until it locks with a click.
    4. Close the cover and wait for the printer to check the cartridges.

    Brother’s steps: Cannot Detect (MFC-J1010DW), Cannot Detect Ink Volume (DCP-J1050DW), or your model on Brother NZ support.
