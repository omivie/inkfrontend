    How to Change the Ink Cartridges in a Canon PIXMA (PG-645 / CL-646)

    Canon PIXMA home printers in the MG2500, MG3000, TS3100 and TR4500 families take two FINE cartridges: PG-645 black and CL-646 colour.

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Changing the cartridges

    1. Switch the printer on and open the front cover (on some models, the paper output tray first).
    2. Wait until the cartridge holder moves to the centre and stops.
    3. Push the empty cartridge down to release it and lift it out. Black (PG) goes in the right-hand slot, colour (CL) on the left; the holder is labelled.
    4. Remove the new cartridge’s protective tape. Do not touch the gold contacts or the nozzles.
    5. Slide the cartridge in at a slight upward angle and push it up until it clicks.
    6. Close the cover. The printer may run a short clean before it is ready.

    NZ cartridges are region-coded

    Canon FINE cartridges only work in printers from the same region. A New Zealand PG-645 printer takes PG-645 and PG-645XL, not the PG-245 or PG-545 sold for other markets, even though they look the same. Buy the code printed on your old cartridge.

    Standard or XL?

    XL cartridges hold more ink for the same slot. From our product data: PG-645 black about 180 pages, PG-645XL black about 400 pages. Colour: CL-646 and CL-646XL. Both sizes fit the same printers listed with XL above. More in how long does a cartridge last?
