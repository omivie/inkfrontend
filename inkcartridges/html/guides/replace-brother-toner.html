    How to Replace the Toner in a Brother Laser Printer

    Most Brother laser printers hold the toner cartridge inside a drum unit. You change the toner on its own, far more often than the drum. This guide shows which toner your printer takes, how to fit it, and what to do when the message does not clear.

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Mono printers: fitting a new toner

    1. Switch the printer on and open the front cover.
    2. Lift out the drum and toner assembly by its handle.
    3. Push down the green lock lever on the drum and lift the old toner cartridge out.
    4. Unpack the new toner and shake it gently from side to side a few times to spread the toner. Remove the protective cover.
    5. Push the new toner into the drum until it clicks and the green lever lifts by itself.
    6. Clean the corona wire: slide the green tab on the drum from left to right a few times, then return it to its home position (the ▲ mark). A tab left mid-way prints a vertical stripe.
    7. Slide the assembly back into the printer and close the cover.

    Colour printers: one slot per colour

    Colour models such as the HL-L3240CDW hold four toner cartridges on a tray. Pull the tray out, lift out the colour that is empty (each slot is labelled), fit the new cartridge in the same slot, and push the tray back in. Match the colour to the label on the slot, not to the position.

    "Replace Toner" still shows after a new cartridge

    • Check the cartridge is pushed fully into the drum (the lever must lift) and that the protective cover is off.
    • Check the corona wire tab is back at its home position.
    • If the message stays, follow Brother’s steps for your model. Button sequences differ from model to model, so we do not repeat one here: Brother: toner message still displayed (HL-L3240CDW), or find your model on Brother NZ support.
    • If a cartridge you bought from us is faulty or still is not recognised after these checks, contact us and see our returns policy.

    Toner or drum?

    Faint pages, or marks that repeat at the same spot down the page, can mean the drum is worn rather than the toner empty. The printer also shows "Drum End Soon" or "Replace Drum". See how to replace a Brother drum unit.
