    What Ink Does My Printer Use?

    Two numbers tell you which cartridge to buy: your printer’s model number and the code on the cartridge it takes now. Either one is enough to find the right cartridge here.

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Find the model number

    • Brother: on the front or top panel, for example MFC-L2713DW or DCP-J1050DW.
    • Canon: on the front, next to "PIXMA", for example TS3160. Include the PIXMA series letters.
    • Epson: on the top or front, for example ET-2810 (EcoTank) or XP-2200.
    • HP: on the front or under the cartridge door, for example DeskJet 2820e. The longer product number on the back label works too.

    Search by printer

    Type the model into the search box at the top of this page, for example "Brother MFC-L2713DW". When the name matches one printer exactly, you go straight to the cartridges that fit it.

    Read the code on the old cartridge

    The code is printed on the cartridge label, for example TN-2445, LC431XL, PG-645 or HP 67. Search that code to see every cartridge in the family, genuine and compatible.

    Look-alike codes from other countries

    Some makers sell the same cartridge under different codes in different regions, and some region-lock them. A New Zealand Canon PG-645 printer needs PG-645, not PG-245 or PG-545. Buy the code your NZ printer lists, or ask us if your printer came from overseas.

    Genuine or compatible

    Most codes come as both. Not sure whether to buy genuine or compatible? Genuine vs compatible, explained. Still unsure? Contact us with your printer model and we will tell you what fits.
