    How to Refill an Epson EcoTank (522 and 502 Bottles)

    EcoTank printers have refillable tanks instead of cartridges. Each bottle has a keyed nozzle that fits only its own colour’s tank and stops filling at the top line.

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    Refilling a tank

    1. Check the ink level against the lines on the front of the tank unit.
    2. Open the tank unit cover, then the cap of the tank you are filling.
    3. Shake the bottle gently, remove its cap and peel off any seal.
    4. Hold the bottle upright and push its nozzle into the matching tank. It only fits its own colour.
    5. Let it fill. It stops by itself at the upper line; do not squeeze the bottle.
    6. Remove the bottle, close the tank cap firmly and close the cover. Repeat for each colour.

    Model-specific steps and videos: Epson NZ support (ET-2810).

    First fill versus top-up

    • The first fill on a new printer also primes the ink lines, so the printer runs a charging cycle for several minutes. Do not switch it off.
    • When topping up, keep leftover ink in its capped bottle, upright, out of sunlight.
    • Top up before a tank drops below the lower line, to keep air out of the lines.
